Synthesis of Two-Dimensional Antiferromagnetic Semiconductor CuFeS2 with a High Néel Temperature

第五作者5th authorJCR 一区 TopJCR Q1 Top

Di Wang, Yingying Liu, Hua Zhang, Yong Liu, Dan Li, Zucheng Zhang, Ping Lu, Chen Yi, Kun He, Liqiang Zhang, et al.

Nano Letters, 2025, 25(14), 5925–5931 · DOI: 10.1021/acs.nanolett.5c01088

查看原文 ↗Read the paper ↗

研究背景Background

二维反铁磁半导体在自旋电子学与磁光器件中极具应用前景，但已报道的体系普遍存在奈尔温度（TN）低、空气稳定性差的问题，严重限制了实际应用。

Two-dimensional antiferromagnetic (AFM) semiconductors are promising for spintronics and magneto-optical devices, but reported systems generally suffer from low Néel temperatures (TN) and poor air stability, which severely limit practical applications.

研究方法Methods

采用化学气相沉积（CVD）合成二维 CuFeS2（CFS）纳米片；通过电学输运测量确认其半导体特性；利用变温 Raman 与二次谐波（SHG）光谱证实反铁磁序并测定磁相变温度。

2D CuFeS2 (CFS) nanosheets were synthesized by chemical vapor deposition. Their semiconducting nature was confirmed by electrical transport measurements, and the AFM order together with the high magnetic phase-transition temperature was demonstrated by temperature-dependent Raman and second-harmonic generation (SHG) spectroscopy.

主要结论Conclusions

二维 CFS 表现出高达 473.0 ± 0.4 K 的奈尔温度，并可在空气中稳定存在至少两周。该工作提供了一种兼具高 TN 与空气稳定性的新型二维反铁磁半导体，为非常规物性研究与高温自旋电子学开辟了新的材料平台。

The 2D CFS exhibits a high TN of 473.0 ± 0.4 K and remains stable in air for at least two weeks. This work provides a new 2D AFM semiconductor combining high TN with air stability, offering a novel platform for unconventional physics and high-temperature spintronics.
