Energy Barrier Inversion and Kagome Electronic Lattice Induced by Incommensurate Moiré Reconstruction

Dan Li et al.

Nature Communications, 2026（稿件在审）

在审中 · 录用后更新链接Under review · link coming soon

研究背景Background

莫尔超晶格为研究关联电子态与激子物理提供了高度可调的平台。在转角 WSe2/WS2 等半导体异质结中，晶格重构会显著改变莫尔势能面，但非公度（incommensurate）莫尔体系的重构行为及其对激子与电子晶格的影响仍缺乏系统理解。

Moiré superlattices provide a highly tunable platform for correlated electronic states and exciton physics. In twisted semiconductor heterostructures such as WSe2/WS2, lattice reconstruction strongly reshapes the moiré potential landscape, yet the reconstruction behavior of incommensurate moiré systems and its impact on excitons and electronic lattices remain poorly understood.

研究方法Methods

采用机械剥离结合干法转移堆垛技术，可控制备不同转角的 WSe2/WS2 范德华异质结；利用低温光致发光（PL）、偏振分辨光谱与扫描探针表征，并结合原子尺度模拟与理论分析，系统研究非公度莫尔重构对势能面与激子性质的调控规律。

Twisted WSe2/WS2 van der Waals heterostructures were fabricated by mechanical exfoliation and dry-transfer stacking. Low-temperature photoluminescence (PL), polarization-resolved spectroscopy and scanning probe characterization, combined with atomistic simulation and theoretical analysis, were used to systematically investigate how incommensurate moiré reconstruction reshapes the potential landscape and excitonic properties.

主要结论Conclusions

首次发现非公度莫尔重构可诱导莫尔势能垒反转（energy barrier inversion）与莫尔激子圆偏振手性的切换；在特定转角下体系形成 Kagome 电子态晶格，为莫尔量子物态的人工调控提供了全新机制。稿件目前处于 Nature Communications 审稿阶段，更多细节将于发表后更新。

We reveal that incommensurate moiré reconstruction induces an inversion of the moiré energy barrier and a switch of exciton circular polarization; at specific twist angles the system hosts a Kagome electronic lattice, offering a new mechanism for engineering moiré quantum states. The manuscript is currently under review at Nature Communications.
