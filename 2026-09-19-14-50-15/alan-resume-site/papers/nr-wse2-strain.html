Strain Engineering of Emergent Anisotropic Responses in WSe2 Monolayer

合作作者Co-author中科院一区 TopCAS Q1 Top

Yang Du, Yuhao Kong, Shunhui Zhang, Jinhui Cao, Baihui Zhang, Junjie Jin, Yulong Tang, Dan Li, Zhihui Chen, Jun He, et al.

Nano Research, 2026 · DOI: 10.26599/NR.2026.94908725

查看原文 ↗Read the paper ↗

研究背景Background

应变工程是打破二维材料本征对称性、诱导可调各向异性响应的有效策略，但如何通过应变获得新型各向异性行为仍是一个挑战，且缺乏系统研究。

Strain engineering is an effective strategy to break the intrinsic symmetry of two-dimensional materials and enable tunable anisotropic responses. However, achieving novel anisotropic behaviors through strain remains challenging and lacks systematic study.

研究方法Methods

将单层 WSe2 与 SiO2 纳米棒构筑 2D/1D 混合维异质结构，利用纳米棒直径精确调控 WSe2 中的应变；通过偏振分辨光致发光、压电力显微镜（PFM）等手段表征其各向异性光学与电学响应，并构筑光电探测器进行功能验证。

Mixed-dimensional 2D/1D heterostructures were constructed from monolayer WSe2 and SiO2 nanorods, whose diameters precisely regulate the strain in WSe2. Polarization-resolved photoluminescence and piezoresponse force microscopy (PFM) were used to characterize the anisotropic optical and electrical responses, and heterostructure photodetectors were fabricated for functional demonstration.

主要结论Conclusions

应变界面处的 WSe2 表现出可调的光致发光增强、带隙调制与偏振发光，并意外观测到面内铁电性。异质结探测器覆盖 375–808 nm 宽波段，光开关比约 3200，探测率达 2.4×10⁹ Jones，响应速度约 11.3 ms，二向色比在 1–1.51 范围内可调，并可清晰成像字母“H”。该工作为调控 TMDC 各向异性响应并赋予其新功能提供了有效策略。

Strained interfacial WSe2 exhibits tunable PL enhancement, bandgap modulation and polarized emission, with unexpected in-plane ferroelectricity. The photodetectors cover 375–808 nm broadband detection with a photoswitching ratio of ~3200, a detectivity of 2.4×10⁹ Jones, a response time of ~11.3 ms, and a tunable dichroic ratio from 1 to 1.51, reliably imaging the letter "H". This work offers an effective strategy for tailoring anisotropic responses of TMDCs and endowing them with new functionalities.
